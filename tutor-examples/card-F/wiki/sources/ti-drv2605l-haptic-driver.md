# DRV2605L Flexible Haptic and Vibration Driver (TI product page)

**What it is:** A Texas Instruments I2C haptic driver IC for ERM and LRA actuators, with a built-in effect library and closed-loop drive. It is a catalog part you can buy.

**Key excerpt** (verbatim from the page):
> Flexible Haptic and Vibration Driver

**What it shows works:** Supply 2 V to 5.2 V; input by I2C, PWM or analog; works with both ERM and LRA; LRA-only automatic resonance tracking; over 100 effects (6 ERM libraries, 1 LRA library); 0.7 ms startup; 4 µA shutdown; packages DSBGA (9 pin) and VSSOP (10 pin); operating range -40 to 85 °C.

**Relevance to this project:** Informs the drive-circuit choice for a coin ERM or LRA in the ankle band, including battery-powered operation and rhythmic effect sequencing.

**Limitations:** Fetched page is a product summary, not the full datasheet (no motor part numbers or current figures). Effect library depends on licensed Immersion software. 2 V minimum supply bounds single-cell battery designs. Smart-Loop is listed as patent pending.
