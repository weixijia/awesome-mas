# Vibration Perception Thresholds of Skin Mechanoreceptors Are Influenced by Different Contact Forces

**What it is:** Zippenfennig, Wynands, Milani, J Clin Med 2021;10(14):3083. Measured vibration perception thresholds (VPT) at the heel of 10 healthy participants at 30 Hz and 200 Hz, with contact force controlled between 0.3 N and 9.6 N. Content read from the PubMed search snippet and the Europe PMC record (abstract level).

**Key excerpt** (verbatim from the page):
> Significant main effects were found for frequency and contact force.

**What it shows works:** Frequency and contact force each significantly affected VPT, with a significant interaction; force mattered more at 30 Hz. The abstract gives no numeric thresholds.

**Relevance to this project:** Informs how band tightness (motor contact force on skin) can change whether a given vibration frequency is perceived.

**Limitations:** Healthy adults only, heel (sole of foot) not ankle or shank, laboratory vibration exciter rather than a coin/cylinder motor, 10 participants, no PD data.
