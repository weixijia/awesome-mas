# CUE1+ (Parkinson's UK Tech Guide catalogue entry)

**What it is:** A catalogue entry, with a user-panel review, for Charco Neurotech's CUE1+, a chest-mounted vibrotactile cueing wearable registered as a Class I medical device.

**Key excerpt** (verbatim from the page, as reported by the fetch tool):
> a wearable, chest-mounted device that combines the principles of focused vibrotactile stimulation and cueing

**What it shows works:** A commercial vibrotactile cueing wearable exists, about 5 cm x 5 cm x 2.5 cm and roughly 50 g, at 795 GBP plus VAT with a 60 GBP per year adhesive patch fee. Panel found it easy to use and sturdy.

**Relevance to this project:** Shows real-world price, size and user complaints (loud or distracting buzzing, skin irritation from adhesive) for a commercial vibration cueing product.

**Limitations:** Worn on the chest, not the ankle. No motor or driver details, and no detection of freezing of gait is described. Sales were temporarily paused at time of access. Catalogue page, not a clinical trial.
