# Vibrotactile Foot Device for Freezing of Gait in Parkinson's Disease: A Pilot Study

**What it is:** Pilot study (Mov Disord Clin Pract 2024;11(10):1241-1248) of a closed-loop vibration insole-style device: four vibration disks per foot, triggered by insole pressure sensors at foot contact, run from an Android app.

**Key excerpt** (verbatim from the page):
> The results demonstrated 33.1% reduction in number of FOG episodes (P < 0.001)
> 32.6% reduction of freezing episodes (P < 0.001)

**What it shows works:** 33 PD patients with FOG, tested in the "off" medication state: FOG episodes 4.5 vs 3.0 per trial (stim off vs on); percent time frozen 27.9 vs 18.8. Vibration 5-250 Hz, set to 200 Hz. 73% reported at least minimal improvement. The main text attributes the 32.6% figure to percent time frozen.

**Relevance to this project:** Informs the haptic cue component: evidence that vibration cues can reduce freezing in a clinical sample.

**Limitations:** Single center, no control group, no double blinding, fixed order (off first, then on). 42 enrolled; 6 showed no objective freezing and 3 could not perceive the vibration. Cue is foot-contact-triggered, not freeze-prediction, and is on the foot, not ankle band or wrist. Abstract/main text read via PMC; stride-length gain did not survive correction.
