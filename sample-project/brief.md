# Project brief

- **Working title:** StepCue
- **Idea (in the team's words):** A small wearable that notices when a person with Parkinson's is about to "freeze" while walking and gives a rhythmic vibration cue on the wrist or ankle to help them start walking again.
- **Who it is for, and in what situation:** People with Parkinson's who experience freezing of gait, walking at home or outdoors without a carer next to them.
- **One moment of use (what the prototype does):** The user approaches a doorway, their steps shorten and they start to freeze; within a second the band on their ankle pulses in a steady rhythm, and they step through.
- **Technologies or materials imagined:** Motion sensor (accelerometer/gyroscope), small microcontroller, vibration motor, rechargeable battery, soft strap; maybe a phone app to set the rhythm.
- **Least certain technical part:** Detecting a freeze reliably *before* or *as* it happens, in real time, on a tiny device.
